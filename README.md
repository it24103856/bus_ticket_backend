Selected Area: Public Transport  

1) Problem:
   
Public bus transportation is one of the main modes of travel in Sri 
Lanka, but passengers can face difficulties when planning their 
journeys. Information about available buses, routes, departure times, 
fares, and seat availability is often difficult to access in one 
convenient place. Passengers may need to rely on physical bus 
stations, conductors, or different sources of information to identify a 
suitable bus. 
This can result in wasted time, uncertainty when planning journeys, 
difficulty finding suitable buses, and inefficient use of public 
transportation. Therefore, there is a need for a simple digital solution 
that allows passengers to find relevant bus services and plan their 
journeys more conveniently. 

3) Solution:
   
We propose a Sri Lankan Bus Booking System called BusTime.lk 
that provides passengers with a convenient platform to search for and 
book suitable bus services. 
The system allows users to enter their starting location, destination, 
and preferred travel date to find relevant buses. Users can view 
important information such as the bus route, departure time, arrival 
time, fare, and available seats. They can then select an available seat 
and make a booking through the system. 
The system will also provide users with a booking confirmation and 
allow them to view their booking details. By bringing bus information 
and booking functionality into one platform, the proposed system 
aims to reduce the time and uncertainty involved in planning bus 
journeys and encourage more effective use of public transportation in 
Sri Lanka.
